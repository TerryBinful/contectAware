### 7.3 Pre-registered decision rules (docs/Stage2/PREREGISTRATION_FROZEN.md §5), applied mechanically

- **H1** (mechanisms differ in detection delay): Friedman p = <0.001 → supported.
- **H2** (hysteresis faster than the best smoother, Margin): falsified under the pre-registered rule (non-significant or not faster).
- **H3** (hysteresis better than both margin-only and dwell-only): not supported (hysteresis - margin: Holm p = 1, r = 0.07; hysteresis - debounce: Holm p = 1, r = -1.00).
- **Handover framing rule** (trust or SPRT at least as good as hysteresis on both test median detection delay and FAR_frame at the matched point): triggered by SPRT.
- **H4** (M slower than X for every mechanism): not supported (0/9 significant).

These are statements about the pre-registered rules on this benchmark, not a ranking of mechanisms: the tables above show that the mechanisms trade security, genuine-user cost and stability differently.

## 10. Evidence quality and limitations

- PILOT: only a subset of genuine users; statistical tests are underpowered and reported only to exercise the code.
- Identity transitions are **simulated by splicing** frames of different participants; real theft/hand-over (gradual, same device) is not observed.
- Each ExtraSensory participant used **their own phone**. Impostor frames therefore come from a different device, so device-specific sensor characteristics may make impostors easier to detect than on a shared device. Absolute detection numbers are likely optimistic; the comparison between mechanisms consumes the same stream and is less affected.
- Decisions occur about once per minute (1 frame ≈ 60 s); nothing here supports sub-minute claims.
- The cohort is the regular-cadence subset of a student-heavy convenience sample; generalisation beyond it is untested.
- The operating point was matched on validation data; test false-lock rates differ from the target (§6.4).
- Context labels are self-reported and sparse; condition M matches the dominant label only.
- Mechanism grids are finite; a mechanism's result is conditional on its grid (documented in the config).
